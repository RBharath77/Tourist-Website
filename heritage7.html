About Fort St. George

Fort St. George is a historic fortified complex on the Chennai coast and an important part of the city’s colonial-era history. The complex includes historic administrative and religious buildings and the Fort Museum, which presents artefacts connected with the period.

What to look for: Fortifications, historic buildings, museum.
