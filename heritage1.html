About Brihadisvara Temple

A landmark of Chola architecture, the Brihadisvara Temple was built in the early 11th century under Rajaraja I. Its towering vimana, sculptural programme and inscriptions reflect the architectural and cultural achievements of the Chola period. The temple remains an active place of worship and is part of the UNESCO World Heritage property Great Living Chola Temples.

What to look for: Chola architecture, monumental vimana, stone sculpture.
