About Shore Temple

The Shore Temple is one of the signature monuments of Mahabalipuram, an important Pallava-era heritage town. Built in the early 8th century, the structural temple complex faces the Bay of Bengal and showcases the refined stone architecture for which the site is known.

What to look for: Pallava architecture, sea views, stone carvings.
