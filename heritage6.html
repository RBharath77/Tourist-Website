About Gangaikonda Cholapuram Temple

The Brihadisvara Temple at Gangaikondacholapuram was built by Rajendra I and became the centrepiece of his imperial capital. Its elegant vimana and exceptional sculptures demonstrate the continuing development of Chola temple architecture. It is part of the UNESCO Great Living Chola Temples property.

What to look for: Chola sculpture, vimana, historic capital.
