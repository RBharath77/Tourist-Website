About Gingee Fort

Gingee Fort is a large historic fortification spread across rocky hills in northern Tamil Nadu. Its defensive walls, gateways, reservoirs and elevated viewpoints illustrate the strategic importance of the site across several historical periods.

What to look for: Fort walls, hilltops, panoramic views.
