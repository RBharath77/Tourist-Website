About Nilgiri Mountain Railway

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway connects the plains with the hill station of Ooty through a scenic route across the Nilgiris. The journey is valued for its engineering heritage as well as the changing landscapes of forests, valleys and mountain settlements.

What to look for: Mountain train, valleys, hill scenery.
