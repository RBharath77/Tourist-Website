About Airavatesvara Temple

Airavatesvara Temple at Darasuram forms part of the UNESCO-listed Great Living Chola Temples. Built under Rajaraja II in the 12th century, it is noted for its highly ornate execution, detailed sculptures and the distinctive chariot-like front mandapa.

What to look for: Stone carvings, Chola art, mandapa.
