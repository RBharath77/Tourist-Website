A grand 17th-century palace in Madurai known for its monumental courtyard, arches and Indo-Saracenic architectural character.

About Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace

Thirumalai Nayakkar Palace was built in the 17th century during the reign of King Thirumalai Nayak. The surviving complex is noted for its huge pillars, ornate arches, stucco work and dramatic courtyard spaces, making it an important architectural stop in Madurai.

What to look for: Grand courtyard, arches, palace halls.
