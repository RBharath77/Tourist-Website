About Meenakshi Amman Temple

Located in the heart of Madurai, Meenakshi Amman Temple is a major centre of Tamil religious and cultural life. The complex is famous for its richly decorated gopurams, halls, sculptures and long-standing festivals and worship traditions.

What to look for: Gopurams, sculpture, temple corridors.
